ORB descriptor similarity

Uses cv.ORB from OpenCV.js. Every pair of images is compared: for each descriptor find the Hamming distance of the 256 descriptor bits, computed in a separate wasm module built from src/popcnt.ts. Pairs with the most low-distance matches are the most similar.
